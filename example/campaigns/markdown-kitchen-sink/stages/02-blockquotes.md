## Blockquotes

A simple quote:

> The best way to predict the future is to invent it.

Quotes can stack. Add a second `>` to go one level deeper:

> Outer thought.
>
> > A reply, quoted inside the first.
> >
> > > And a third layer, just to prove it works.

## Emphasis

You can combine *italic*, **bold**, and ***bold italic***, plus `inline code`.

## Your answer

A blockquote placed inside another blockquote is called a **nested** blockquote.

The answer is **nested**.
