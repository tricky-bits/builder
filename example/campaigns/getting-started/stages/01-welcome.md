Every stage has a body (this text), an optional set of hints, and an answer box.

To complete a stage you type the answer and submit it. The answer is checked
right here in your browser — it is never sent to a server. Answers are
**case-insensitive** and surrounding spaces are ignored, so `Welcome` and
`welcome ` both work.
