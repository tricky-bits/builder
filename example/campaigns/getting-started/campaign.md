Welcome to **Getting Started** — the gentlest campaign on the site.

Most campaigns ask you to *figure out* the answer. This one does the opposite:
it **tells you the answer** and asks you to type it, so you can learn the
mechanics without any puzzle getting in the way.

What you'll learn:

1. Where the answer appears on a stage.
2. How to submit it.
3. How completing a stage unlocks the next one.

Two short stages. Let's go.
