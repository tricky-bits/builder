This campaign focuses on **stage mechanics** rather than markdown.

- **Stage 1** carries three hints with increasing `wait_seconds` (0, 15, 45), so
  later hints only become available after you've spent some time on the stage.
- **Stage 2** is marked harder (`difficulty: 4`) with a longer `eta_minutes`, to
  show how those badges differ across stages.

Both answers are written in bold, so the timing — not the puzzle — is the point.
