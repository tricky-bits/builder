# Terms & Privacy

This is example content for a demonstration site. It is not legal advice and
describes no real service.

## Privacy

TBB produces a **static site**. There is no backend, no account system, and no
tracking. Any progress you make solving stages is stored **locally in your own
browser** (via `localStorage`) and never leaves your device.

- Clearing your browser data clears your progress.
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- No cookies are set by the builder itself.

## Sharing

You can share a direct link to any stage. Anyone with the link can open it — no
account or prior progress required.

## Fair play

Because answer checking happens in the browser, a determined visitor can find
ways around a puzzle. That is fine for this example — its purpose is to show the
builder, not to guard secrets.
